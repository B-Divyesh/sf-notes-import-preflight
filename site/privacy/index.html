Privacy policy

Understand how your files stay private

Effective 6 September 2026

Command-line scans

The command-line tool reads the path you provide. It does not change source files.

Saved reports contain counts, paths, names, sizes, and fingerprints. They do not contain note bodies or attachment bytes.

Browser folder checks

The browser reads only files you select. It does not upload names, contents, or results.

The sample demo uses bundled data in memory. It does not read or write your saved data.

Licenses and saved baselines

If you paste a license, this site stores it in your browser. Verification sends only that token to Sociobot.

A valid license can save one aggregate baseline in local storage. You can delete it from the Plus workspace.

Tracking

This site has no analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Contact

Open a privacy question in the public issue tracker (external site). Never attach a private export.
