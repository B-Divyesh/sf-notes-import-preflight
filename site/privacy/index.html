Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

Your archive stays yours

The command-line tool runs entirely on your computer. It has no telemetry and makes no network requests. The browser demo reads only files you explicitly select, processes them in memory, and does not upload their contents, names, or results.

License data

If you buy or restore Preflight Plus, this site stores your license token and the time and result of its latest verification in your browser’s local storage. Verification sends the token to Sociobot’s billing API at most once per day. It does not send archive data.

Payments and support

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and processes checkout, payment, refunds, and the email needed to deliver a license. Their policies apply on the hosted checkout. If you contact support, we use your message only to answer your request.

Analytics and retention

There are no advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove the locally saved license. Export reports are saved only when and where you choose.

Contact

Privacy questions can be opened in the project’s public issue tracker; do not attach private archives.
